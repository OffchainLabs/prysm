### Fixed

- Fixed backfill wedging at 100% without ever reporting completion: `p2pBatchWorkerPool.complete` could block forever waiting for worker traffic while end-of-sequence batches trickled in one per scheduling pass, leaving the service parked after the final import and `WaitForCompletion` (which the pruner waits on) never returning. The pool now counts batches handed out but not yet returned, and `complete` ends the sequence synchronously as soon as an end-of-sequence sentinel is present with no batches outstanding, instead of blocking until one sentinel per worker had accumulated. This also removes a data race on the pool's `endSeq` slice, which was appended from the router goroutine while read from the service goroutine; expired batches are now handed back to the service goroutine through the pool instead.
