### Fixed

- Fixed backfill reaching the end of the block history without ever reporting completion, which left the database pruner waiting forever on the backfill service. The worker pool ended its sequence only after collecting one end-of-sequence batch per worker, but the runloop hands out at most one of those per turn and turns are driven by finished batches from workers, so a node that imported its last batch on the same turn it produced the first signal blocked forever with nothing left in flight. The pool now tracks the batches it has handed out but not yet returned, and ends the sequence as soon as it has an end-of-sequence batch and nothing outstanding. This also removes a data race on the pool's end-of-sequence state, which the router goroutine used to mutate while the service goroutine read it; batches the router retires because they fell outside the retention window are now handed back to the service like any other result.
- Fixed backfill stopping silently when a batch failed with an error it could not recover from. Such a batch is never requested again and the older batches depend on it to chain parent roots, so the service now ends with that error instead of waiting for progress that cannot happen, and `WaitForCompletion` reports it to waiters like the pruner rather than reporting a successful completion. Every other exit from the backfill service unblocks `WaitForCompletion` as well, so a service that fails to start no longer leaves the pruner parked until shutdown.
